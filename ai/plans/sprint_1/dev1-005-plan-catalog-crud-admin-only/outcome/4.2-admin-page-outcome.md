- **Metadata**: mirrors the established locale-aware dashboard pattern (`profile/page.tsx` — `generateMetadata()` reading the `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie), deriving `title`/`description` from `plansTranslations.pageTitle`/`pageSubtitle`.
