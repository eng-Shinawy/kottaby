### I.2 The fix (files + key lines)

- **`scripts/launch/launch-waiver-signatories.ts`** (NEW) — the doc-ingestion parser.
  `parseWaiverSignatories(rowIds, docPath)` reads `docs/planning/PRODUCTION_READINESS.md`, finds each

  doc; the parser never writes, defaults, or infers.

- **`scripts/launch/launch-gate-dryrun.ts`** — `validateSampleReport` uses the same

  block the sample verdict" check is now conditional on every ingested signatory being empty, and a
  new no-fabrication check asserts each serialized signatory equals the ingested record exactly.
- **`.md` evidence existence-only pin UNTOUCHED** — `evidenceArtifactVerdict` still applies its
  content check to `.json` artifacts only; DOC-SIGNOFF/MANUAL-DRILL rows remain existence-marked and
