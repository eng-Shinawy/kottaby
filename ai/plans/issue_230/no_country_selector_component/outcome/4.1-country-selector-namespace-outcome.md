## Summary

Registered the `countrySelector` compile-time i18n namespace by following the
6-step namespace registration recipe (`shared/AGENTS.md:201-216`) exactly, with
the `wallet` namespace as the live exemplar (per `plan-review-R1.md` F-11/F-12).
The namespace exposes exactly three static labels — `placeholder`,
`noOptionsText`, `fieldLabel` — with full en/ar parity enforced at compile time
(both leaf consts typed `CountrySelectorLabels`) and at runtime by a colocated
parity test.

**Namespace handle:** `CountrySelector` — id string **`countrySelector.countrySelector`**
**Bundle key:** `countrySelectorTranslations: CountrySelectorLabels`

| Key | en | ar |
|---|---|---|
| `placeholder` | `"Select your country"` | `"اختر دولتك"` |
| `noOptionsText` | `"No country found"` | `"لا توجد دولة مطابقة"` |
| `fieldLabel` | `"Country"` | `"الدولة"` |

Arabic copy was verified against the existing `ar` files' tone: `الدولة` is the
codebase-wide word for the country field (`auth.country`, `dashboard.country`,
`adminUsers`/`adminTeachers`/`adminStudents` columns, `adminBroadcasts.countryLabel`),
and the empty-state phrasing follows the prevailing `لا توجد …` construction
(`common.noResults: "لا توجد نتائج"`, wallet/ledger empty states). The suggested
`بلد`/`لم نعثر` variants were rejected in favor of this tone-consistent wording.
