- **Blocked** (glyph-form disallowed here) — Not resolved, plan cannot complete until addressed; this state is FORBIDDEN in this ledger — record findings as RESOLVED-REFERENCE with an owning ticket instead
- 🔄 **In Progress** — Currently being worked on
- 📅 **Forward** — Pre-seeded forward item owned by a later ticket; non-blocking for this plan
