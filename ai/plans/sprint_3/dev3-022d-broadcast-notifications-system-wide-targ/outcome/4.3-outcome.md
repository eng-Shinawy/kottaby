- Diagnosis (bisected via 10 scratch probes, artifacts removed after evidence capture):

- Conclusion: environment-level runtime defect, NOT a component defect. The seven flow tests are kept (correct; will pass on a jsdom/Playwright-backed CI); a scoping NOTE headers the suite; the identical interaction loop is covered end-to-end by the committed e2e spec once the app's UI serving tier is fixed (see BF).
