## 3. TTL semantics

- The cache owns **no TTL policy**: `ttlSeconds` is propagated as-is into `EX` for both `claim` and `store` (Tier 2 pins `NOTIFICATION_EMIT_CLAIM_TTL_SECONDS = 86_400` — the engine's 24h claim window, `emit-idempotency.ts:30` — and a custom `60`).
- `claim` always carries the `NX` token; `store` never does (a held key must accept the post-commit receipt overwrite; pinned in Tier 2 via the recorded `nx: "NX" | undefined` field).
- Claimed-but-unstored keys hold the `"1"` sentinel (`CLAIM_SENTINEL_VALUE`) under the same TTL — the engine reads that state as "held, no replayable receipt" → `unavailable` → fail-open.
