This contradicts the module's own documented contract ("Malformed drafts normalize to 'unfiltered' instead of erroring", `audit-trail-filters.ts:16`) and the route's documented silent-drop posture. Suggested repair (out of this review's scope): apply the same `≥1` bound and inverted-pair drop in `appliedFiltersFromDrafts` (or clamp in `buildFiltersInput`), and set `htmlInput min: 1` — see next bullet.
