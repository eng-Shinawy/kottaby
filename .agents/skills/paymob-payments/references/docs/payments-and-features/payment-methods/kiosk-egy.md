# Kiosk [EGY]
**Who is this for** \- Product managers, business owners, and stakeholders who want to understand kiosk payments

**Outcome** \- Understand how kiosk payments work and the end-to-end customer payment experience

* * *

Paymob enables you to accept kiosk payments, allowing customers to generate a payment reference and complete the payment in cash at supported kiosk locations.

![](https://d24lr4zqs1tgqh.cloudfront.net/e0548217-f79e-43e0-8301-cf6cd5b45e54.jpg)

![](https://d24lr4zqs1tgqh.cloudfront.net/4c391af4-e34d-484a-8243-47449c23afab-696e9edc0acf4f5a50bce69f.svg)

### Supported for Regions

It's supported in the region **EGY** only

![](https://d24lr4zqs1tgqh.cloudfront.net/152cfcfe-1e2b-4458-8b7c-bf8349a964f8-696e9edc0acf4f5a50bce69f.svg)

### Supported Payment Actions

This payment methods doesn't support **refunds**.

![](https://d24lr4zqs1tgqh.cloudfront.net/17372e33-9f46-472f-8ebf-1419d5f3c114-696e9edc0acf4f5a50bce69f.svg)

  1. [**APIs**](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/integration-paths/apis)

  2. [**Plugins**](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/integration-paths/plugins)

> 
> The **Kiosk** payment method can have both **test** and **Live** integration IDs.
