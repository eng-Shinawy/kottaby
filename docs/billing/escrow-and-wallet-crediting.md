# Session Fee Escrow & Teacher Wallet Crediting — Canonical Reference

**Domain:** Billing & Wallets (session-fee escrow + teacher earning ledger)
**Status:** Implemented and verified (hold-as-debit at booking, single-winner settle/release, exactly-once earning credit)
**Source of truth for:** the escrow pairing between the student's held lane and the teacher's wallet, the settle/release/race matrix over `sessions.fee_held`, the INV-W*/INV-B4/INV-S3 invariant map with schema + code + test anchors, the deferrals that shape the money path, and the boundary hand-offs every dependent billing ticket must honor.

This document consolidates the money-side of the session lifecycle. Downstream consumers (withdrawals, admin adjustments, re-evaluation deductions, financial verification) MUST read it before touching `sessions.fee_held`, `sessions.held_balance_lane`, `wallet.*`, or `teacher_transaction.*`. The hold is written exactly once per booking, consumed exactly once per settlement, and refunded at most once per release — the guarded primitives live in `StudentRepository`, `SessionRepository`, and `WalletRepository` and are composed by `SessionLifecycleService`; consumers extend them, never re-implement them.

---

## 1. Why

A booked session is a two-sided money contract: the student funds a hold from an allowance lane at request time, and the teacher receives the platform fee only when the lesson is actually attended and confirmed. The domain has to be race-proof (two concurrent bookings on a one-unit lane must settle as exactly one session and one debit; a confirm racing a timeout sweep must produce exactly ONE financial outcome), exactly-once (one hold ⇒ at most one earning ledger row and one wallet increment), and fail-closed (a zero-balance denial commits zero rows; an unreadable provenance lane refuses to refund rather than guessing). Each rule below exists because a specific failure mode was proven or ruled out by the executing suites — the worst of them is *re-timing the debit*: moving the decrement to completion re-opens the double-booking race the escrow exists to kill. Read §2 and §5 before adding any writer over these columns, and §6 before touching any test that asserts them.

## 2. Hold-As-Debit Semantics (the binding ruling)

**"Hold" = a guarded debit of one unit lane at request time** — not a balance freeze with a later decrement. The ticket prose once described a balance "held (not decremented yet)" at request and "decremented by 1" at dual confirmation; that wording is superseded (canonical rulings: `docs/sessions/session-lifecycle.md` §4–§5; `docs/billing/segregated-session-balance.md` §6, decision D1). The shipped and re-verified semantics:

1. **Debit ladder at booking (trial-first):** the creation transaction attempts a guarded `UPDATE students SET balance_trial = balance_trial - 1 … WHERE balance_trial > 0 RETURNING id` and, on a miss, the same guarded statement on the intent lane (`balance_hifz` for Hifz, `balance_tajweed` for Tajweed) — `debitBookingLadder` (`backend/services/classes/session-lifecycle.booking.ts:127-159`; trial leg :133-136, intent leg :137-158). An expired-uncovered lane is denied `SUBSCRIPTION_EXPIRED` before the balance denial (:140-149). Both legs missing → `ValidationError("INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE", t.insufficientBalance)` (:156) and the whole transaction rolls back with zero writes.
2. **The guarded primitive IS the lock:** `StudentRepository.decrementLaneIfAvailable` fuses the guard into the statement — `WHERE id = ? AND <lane> > 0 RETURNING id` (`backend/db/repo/students/student.repository.ts:404-419`, guard predicate :415). Zero TOCTOU window; a concurrent one-unit race settles as exactly one fulfillment and one localized denial.
3. **Hold + provenance on the session row:** the insert carries the platform fee, `feeHeld: true`, the winning lane, and the 24h confirmation deadline (`session-lifecycle.booking.ts:182-185`; columns `backend/db/schema/classes/session.ts:63-65,70`). `held_balance_lane` is the row's PERMANENT refund provenance — NULL only while no fee has ever been held, never rewritten or nulled afterwards (schema docblock `backend/db/schema/classes/session.ts:32-39`). The fee itself is platform-owned and never client input: `sessionFeeForIntent` (`backend/services/classes/session-lifecycle.guards.ts:83-85`) reads the constants `SESSION_FEE_HIFZ` / `SESSION_FEE_TAJWEED` = `"25.00"`, `SESSION_FEE_CURRENCY = "EGP"` (`shared/constants/session-fees.constants.ts:27-33`).
4. **Settlement is a flip, not a debit:** dual confirmation runs ONE guarded UPDATE that stamps the student confirmation AND sets `fee_held = false` — `SessionRepository.confirmStudentCompletionOnce` (`backend/db/repo/classes/session.repository.ts:433-455`; predicate `fee_held = true` ∧ status `completed` ∧ teacher stamp present ∧ student stamp absent, :447-452). The teacher's wallet credit composes inside the SAME transaction (`confirmCompletionInTx`, `backend/services/classes/session-lifecycle.confirmation.ts:112-139`; credit call :129). Net effect per attended session: exactly −1 on the funding lane — never −2.
5. **Release re-increments the SAME lane:** cancellation, timeout sweep, and arbitration-cancel all funnel through `refundHeldLaneToProvenance` (`backend/services/classes/session-lifecycle.transitions.ts:222-237`) → `StudentRepository.incrementLane` (`backend/db/repo/students/student.repository.ts:433-441`) — always to the recorded `held_balance_lane`, only when it is non-null (:227-229), and fail-closed on an unreadable value (:230-235, the refusal rolls the whole cancellation back). A NULL-lane legacy row refunds nothing, which is correct: no hold exists.
6. **Two units, one reconciliation point.** The student side moves integer lane units (±1, never currency); the teacher side moves EGP decimal strings (`wallet.balance`/`wallet.total_earning`, `backend/db/schema/billing/wallet.ts:27-28`). The two sides are reconciled ONLY by the settlement event: one dual-confirmation settlement hold ⇒ exactly one `earning` ledger row whose `amount` is the session's fee string taken VERBATIM — never re-parsed or re-rounded (`WalletRepository.creditEarningOnce`, `backend/db/repo/billing/wallet.repository.ts:78-110`; verbatim rule in the :70-74 docblock, amount inserted :94). Release paths touch only lanes: zero ledger rows, wallet untouched (§3.3).

### 3.2 Side effects per transition

| Transition | Ledger / wallet writes | Notifications | Exactly-once arbiter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Book | `session` insert; lane −1; idempotency claim | teacher request notice | header-carried `X-Idempotency-Key` → `ctx.idempotencyKey` (`session-lifecycle.mutation.ts:135-139`); missing key is a pre-DB `VALIDATION` (`backend/graphql/test/session-lifecycle-mutations.test.ts:395`); replay THROWS `DUPLICATE_REQUEST` with zero rows (`session-lifecycle.booking.ts:211-224`, wire :404) |
| Settle | `teacher_transaction(earning, completed)` INSERT + guarded wallet increment (both columns) | earning surfaces via `myWallet` | the `fee_held = true` statement predicate (`session.repository.ts:448`) — a second confirm matches zero rows |

The two sides use different units BY DESIGN (§2.6) and are never arithmetically compared outside settlement. The end-to-end money audit — does every earning trace to a consumed hold, and every refund to a released hold — is the Financial Safety Verification ticket's acceptance surface (`docs/planning/TICKETS.md:2985-3026`, §8).

## 5. Rules for Extending the Money Path

1. **Extend the guarded primitives; never re-implement them.** The only writers over a session-escrow lane balance are `decrementLaneIfAvailable` / `incrementLane` (the registration grant path has its own guarded writer — `StudentRepository.grantFreeTrialOnce` directly increments `balance_trial` via `StudentTrialService.grantFreeTrial`); the only earning writer is `creditEarningOnce`; the only settle is `confirmStudentCompletionOnce`; the only release-refund is `refundHeldLaneToProvenance`. New flows compose these inside their own transaction.
2. **The guarded predicate is the lock.** Never branch a money write off a pre-read state. Probe reads classify errors only (`session-lifecycle.confirmation.ts:135-138`); the UPDATE's WHERE clause re-asserts the state it is about to consume.
3. **Share the caller's transaction.** Settlement composes stamp + credit in ONE transaction (`session-lifecycle.service.ts:732` → `confirmCompletionInTx`); there is no post-commit hook and no queue. A rolled-back flow leaves zero rows everywhere.
4. **Money is a decimal string, verbatim.** The fee is inserted, stored, credited, and surfaced without ever being parsed, rounded, or re-derived (`wallet.repository.ts:70-74` docblock, amount :94; string-boundary test `session-lifecycle.service.test.ts:1109`).
5. **Release writes no ledger row.** If a new release-shaped transition appears (cancel variant, sweep variant, arbitration variant), it must route through `refundHeldLaneToProvenance` and must NOT insert a `teacher_transaction`.
6. **Reuse the localized denial keys.** `insufficientBalance` is the shared funds-denial key (booking and withdrawal both assert it through `expectDomainDenial` — `wallet.service.test.ts:224`, `session-lifecycle.service.test.ts:477`); do not mint per-domain copies of a funds denial.
7. **Wallet identity is ctx-derived.** `myWallet` takes zero arguments and resolves `ctx.user.id` (`backend/graphql/query/billing/wallet.query.ts:46-57,64`); `requestWithdrawal` binds the caller id and accepts an amount-ONLY input (`backend/graphql/mutation/billing/wallet.mutation.ts:46-50,75`). No mutation on the teacher self-service surface (`myWallet` / `requestWithdrawal`) accepts a wallet id or a balance value on the wire; administrative adjustment is the explicit ADMIN-governed exception (`adjustTeacherWallet` input `{teacherId, amount, direction, reason}`, `backend/graphql/pothos/admin/admin-finance.pothos.ts:170-177`) — not a self-service input.
8. **New SDL grows only through the pinned surface flow.** `Session.fee` (nullable string) and `Session.feeHeld` (non-null boolean) are exposed at `backend/graphql/pothos/classes/session.pothos.ts:170,174-175`; the wallet/ledger objects live at `backend/graphql/pothos/billing/wallet.pothos.ts:91,126` with the service-capped newest-first ledger page (:145-148). The committed SDL artifact is kept byte-identical to the built schema by the codegen-sync pin.

## 6. Anti-Patterns (What NOT to do)

- **Never re-time the debit.** A "decrement at completion" refactor re-opens the double-booking race (§2, ruling sources). Release flips `fee_held` and refunds the lane; it never debits anything.
- **Never credit a wallet outside `creditEarningOnce`, or outside the confirmation transaction.** Two writes for one settlement (a ledger row without the increment, or the reverse) is the classic split-brain; both writes share one transaction and one guarded statement each.
- **Never let the sweeper or a refund path write a ledger row.** Release economics are lane-side only (§3.2); a release-time earning row would double-pay.
- **Never refund to a guessed lane, and never rewrite/null `held_balance_lane` after it is placed** — it is permanent provenance (`backend/db/schema/classes/session.ts:32-39`); an unreadable value must fail closed, not fall back.
- **Never parse money into floats or re-derive an amount.** Decimal strings end-to-end (§5.4).
- **Never accept a balance, lane name, or fee on the wire.** `SessionSubmitInput` is exactly `{ teacherId, intent }` (`backend/types/classes/session.types.ts:39-42`) and the mutation maps it field-by-field (`session-lifecycle.mutation.ts:127-131`) — mass-assignment of price is structurally impossible.
- **Never hand-edit ledger rows in tests.** `teacher_transaction` UPDATE/DELETE raise at the DB layer (`backend/db/migration/3-immutability-triggers.sql:87-113`); the ONLY sanctioned test cleanup rides `withImmutabilityTriggersSuspended(["teacher_transaction"])` (`test/helpers/db-cleanup.ts:229`).
- **Never replace the guarded predicate with an event/bookkeeping table.** Exactly-once rests on the `fee_held = true` predicate plus the claim table for requests (§3.3); a parallel mechanism would create a second arbiter to keep in sync (see D1, §8).

## 7. GraphQL Surface & Security Posture

- **Role gates (BFLA):** `createSession` is STUDENT-only via an explicit `$all` conjunction (`session-lifecycle.mutation.ts:98-103`); `confirmSessionCompletion` is authenticated with the participant predicate living service-side (`:306-308`); `resolveSessionDispute` is ADMIN-only (`:270-275`) with a service-side governance re-assertion (`session-lifecycle.service.ts:624`); `myWallet` / `requestWithdrawal` are TEACHER-only (`wallet.query.ts:52-57`; `wallet.mutation.ts:62-67`).
- **Oracle-safe denials (BOLA):** for every verb a missing row and a foreign row are indistinguishable to the caller, arm by arm: the confirm arm throws the localized `SESSION_NOT_FOUND` via `rejectUnknownCaller` (`session-lifecycle.confirmation.ts:66-76`) fed by the classifier (:135-138); the cancel/dispute arms route zero-row and foreign-caller misses through `rejectTransitionMiss` → `rejectSessionNotFound` (`session-lifecycle.transitions.ts:76-87,161-174`); the read arm null-folds both cases to the identical `null` (`getSessionById`, `session-lifecycle.queries.ts:41-58`). Proven end-to-end by the foreign-caller journey legs (`session-dual-confirmation.journey.test.ts:492`; `session-lifecycle-denials.journey.test.ts:319`).
- **Server-owned pricing (BOPLA):** §6, rows 6–7.
- **Query-depth / complexity posture (as shipped):** there is **no global GraphQL depth- or complexity-limiting ValidationRule**. The real compensating guards are the transport body cap `MAX_GRAPHQL_BODY_BYTES = 2_000_000` before the engine (`backend/lib/gateway/transport-guard.ts:49`, enforcement `assertWithinBodyLimit` :108-113) and introspection disabled in production (`app/api/graphql/route.ts:49`). The body cap is a coarse **payload-size** bound only — NOT an execution-cost control: array batching and aliases can repeat root selections and resolver work within a single ≤2 MB request, and authentication, pagination, and flat SDL shapes do not bound repeated executions. A rate-limit mechanism also exists on the route (`app/api/graphql/route.ts:26`; posture documented at `app/api/graphql/rate-limit.ts:16`), but it is currently a **fail-open stub that always allows** (`backend/lib/ratelimit.ts:75-87`) — requests are **not** effectively rate-limited today, so it must not be counted as an active guard. Shape-level SDL pins prove flat bounded objects. The money mutations are single-entity guarded operations with flat argument shapes, so there is no unbounded nesting channel over the escrow surface — but any belt-and-braces depth limiter (and a real rate limiter) is a decision for the Financial Safety Verification ticket (§8, D4), not something to assume exists.
- **Known-dirty corner of the SDL freeze (as shipped):** the frozen whole-root operation inventories in `backend/graphql/test/schema-surface.test.ts:747` (mutation-set pin) and `backend/graphql/test/sdl-static-assertions.test.ts:354-356` (frozen mutation list) and :359-361 (frozen query list) lag behind root fields shipped by OTHER surfaces (`purchaseVerificationPlan`; `adjustTeacherWallet` / `approveWithdrawal` / `rejectWithdrawal`; the `admin*` finance queries) — refreshing those pins is surface-freeze housekeeping owned outside this domain. Every escrow/wallet-specific pin passes, including the `myWallet`/`WALLET_QUERY_FIELDS` additions pin (`schema-surface.test.ts:644-688`) and the committed-SDL contains-pins (:1883-1898), and the codegen-sync check proves the committed artifact matches the built schema.

3. **Financial Safety Verification** (`docs/planning/TICKETS.md:2985-3026`) — the adversarial layer over this substrate: double-spend races, escrow-integrity partitions, ledger immutability attacks, and the wallet-identity recompute (`balance = Σcompleted earnings − Σcompleted withdrawals`). Its probes (`docs/billing/financial-safety-verification.md` §2) already cover the double-spend and replay chaos anchored in §3.3; it also owns the D1 runtime-key decision and the D4 depth-limit decision. If a probe fails, the finding is a defect escalated and fixed minimally — the invariants in §4 are permanent CI-enforced contracts, not prose.
